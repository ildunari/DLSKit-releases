# DLSKit Privacy Policy

Effective 27 November 2026.

DLSKit does not collect, store or share any personal data, and it does not track you.

- **Your files stay on your device.** DLSKit reads only the measurement files and projects you open, and it writes only where you choose to save or export. Nothing is uploaded.
- **No accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking.** DLSKit contains no third-party analytics or advertising code.
- **Settings stay on your device.** Preferences such as units and plot options are kept in the app's local settings.
- **No network connections from the App Store app.** The iPhone, iPad and App Store versions of DLSKit make no network connections. The Help and Privacy Policy links open in your web browser.
- **Mac download updates.** The Mac version downloaded from this repository checks GitHub for updates with [Sparkle](https://sparkle-project.org), unless you turn that off in Settings > Updates. That request goes to GitHub, which receives your IP address and the app version as part of an ordinary web request, under [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). DLSKit sends no other information with it.

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/ildunari/DLSKit-releases/issues.
